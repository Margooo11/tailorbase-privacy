Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. About Tailor Base

Tailor Base is a mobile application that generates custom sewing patterns (bodice, sleeve, and skirt) based on your body measurements.

2. Information We Collect

  • Email address — collected when you sign in with Google.
  • Body measurements — entered by you and optionally saved to the cloud (only if you choose to save them).
  • Pattern generation history — scope, date, and format of patterns you generate (stored per user account).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate personalised sewing patterns.
  • To save and retrieve your named measurement sets across sessions.
  • To track your credit balance for pattern generation.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage), hosted in the European Union (europe-west1 region). Google's data processing terms apply: firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties, except Google Firebase which is used solely to provide the service.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us (see section 9). Upon request, all data associated with your account will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

7. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with Firebase security rules that restrict access to your own account only.

8. Children's Privacy

Tailor Base is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.
