Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. About Tailor Base

Tailor Base is a mobile application that generates custom sewing patterns (bodice, sleeve, skirt, dress and trousers) based on your body measurements.

The Tailor Base Patterns app in ChatGPT

Tailor Base Patterns is also available as an app inside ChatGPT. It drafts sewing patterns from the choices and measurements you give in the conversation. You can preview a watermarked pattern without an account. If you link your Tailor Base account (with email and password, or with Google), you can use one credit to download the printable pattern, see your credit balance, and save and reuse your measurements. The ChatGPT app contains no advertising.

  • Without an account, the choices and measurements you give are used only to draw that preview and are not stored by us.
  • Our server (Google Cloud Run, europe-west1) keeps standard request logs, including IP address and user agent. We use IP addresses to limit free previews and to protect the service from abuse. Logs are kept for 30 days.
  • Sign-in tokens for the ChatGPT app are encrypted and are not stored in a database.
  • OpenAI. Your conversation takes place in ChatGPT. What you type there is handled by OpenAI under OpenAI's own privacy policy, which we do not control.

2. Information We Collect

  • Email address — collected when you sign in with Google or with email and password.
  • Body measurements — entered by you and optionally saved to the cloud (only if you choose to save them).
  • Pattern generation history — scope, date, and format of patterns you generate (stored per user account).
  • Advertising identifier & device data (mobile app only) — collected by Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to serve ads within the app, and to personalize them where you've given consent.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate personalised sewing patterns.
  • To save and retrieve your named measurement sets across sessions.
  • To track your credit balance for pattern generation.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage), hosted in the European Union (europe-west1 region). Google's data processing terms apply: firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data with the following third parties, solely to provide the service:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, and file storage.
  • Stripe — processes payments when you buy credits. We do not receive your card details.
  • OpenAI (ChatGPT) — hosts the conversation in which you use the Tailor Base Patterns app in ChatGPT.
  • Google AdMob (mobile app only) — displays ads within the app. AdMob may collect your advertising identifier and other device data to serve and measure ads, and, where you consent via the in-app consent prompt (shown in the EEA and UK), to personalize them. You can manage ad personalization at any time at adssettings.google.com. Learn more about how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us (see section 9). Upon request, all data associated with your account will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

7. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with Firebase security rules that restrict access to your own account only.

8. Children's Privacy

Tailor Base is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
